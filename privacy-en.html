Uafo Privacy Policy

Updated: November 20, 2026 · Applies to the version of Uafo supplied with this policy.

Uafo is provided and maintained by the individual developer 祖刚 宋. No account registration is required, and configurations are mainly stored on your device. Weather requests, Apple membership purchases and restoration, sharing or contacting us at your initiative, and external web visits involve the third-party services described below.

1. Information processed locally

To create and display widgets, the app processes your main text, captions, nicknames, profile introductions, proverbs, relationship start dates, important dates, daily income, work or school schedules, and selected templates, artworks, sizes, units and display options on your device. Selected personal photos, device step counts obtained after you enable activity display, and weather location and results are handled locally as explained below. Each saved instance has an identifier for widget binding; it is not used for advertising tracking. Gallery browsing order and position are stored in the app’s local preferences.

The current app does not upload these configurations, photos or step counts to developer servers. It has no account system, developer cloud backup or account-based cross-device sync, third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking based on this content. We do not actively read contacts, microphones, the HealthKit health database or system calendar events. Art Calendar displays calculated dates. Minimal reads device steps through Motion & Fitness, as explained in section 3.

2. Weather and location

When you choose to fetch weather, the app requests location access while in use and obtains coordinates and a location timestamp from the system to query Apple WeatherKit. The app requests approximately kilometer-level accuracy, but actual coordinate precision depends on the device and permission settings. Location should therefore be managed as sensitive information.

After you save Art Weather, or Art Exhibition or Minimal with real weather enabled, coordinates, the location timestamp and display name, weather results and update times are stored in the local shared configuration for use by the app and widgets. Widgets may continue querying the saved location while the app is closed; they do not continuously track your route. To change the saved location after moving, fetch and save it again in the app. Widgets request updates approximately every 30 minutes, subject to iOS scheduling. Two hours is the threshold for marking weather as old; it does not delete the location or stop requests for the saved coordinates. The extension also stores recent successful weather in its own purgeable local cache.

Requested coordinates are sent to Apple’s weather service. Downloading official attribution assets also requires network requests. The developer does not independently receive your location or build a location database. Apple handles service data under its own rules; see Apple’s Privacy Policy and the sources and legal information linked in weather attribution.

Denying location access does not affect other local templates. You may revoke access in iOS Location Services. Revocation prevents new location acquisition, but does not automatically delete saved coordinates or necessarily stop weather requests using them. In Art Exhibition and Minimal, turn off real weather and save to stop future queries for that configuration; doing so does not automatically delete the saved location. To delete location information from current app configurations, delete every Art Weather, Art Exhibition and Minimal configuration containing it in My Widgets, and remove unused Home Screen widgets. Purgeable extension and system display caches may retain old values temporarily. Deleting a configuration does not promise immediate removal of all system copies.

3. Personal photos, steps and system sharing

Photo features also apply to supported templates such as Her Stories, Lifet and Art Stickers. Art Stickers combines selected photos with bundled stickers locally and saves selection, position, scale and other layout parameters. Imported photos follow the resizing, re-encoding and local storage process below. The developer does not send selected photos to generative AI services or use them to train models. Existing AI-assisted art assets were generated during content production, separately from user photo processing.

Vintage Profile, Personal Profile and Longing let you add, replace or remove specified photos through the iOS system photo picker. The app reads only selected items and does not require full-library access. On the device, selected photos are oriented and resized to a maximum long edge of 600 pixels, re-encoded as JPEG files no larger than 300 KB, and stripped of original metadata. Saving writes the photo into that widget instance’s shared configuration for the app and widgets. Canceling unsaved edits does not write them to shared storage. Removing a photo and saving, or deleting its configuration, removes that configuration’s photo; other instances and originals in Photos are unaffected.

When you enable step display in Minimal, Apple Core Motion’s CMPedometer requests Motion & Fitness access and reads this device’s cumulative steps for the current day and the update time. It does not read HealthKit. Saved values enter that instance’s local shared configuration, and widgets attempt updates when the system permits. Real-time accuracy or success on every refresh is not guaranteed. Steps are not sent to the developer or used for advertising, insurance or medical judgments. Turn step display off and save to clear steps and timestamps from that configuration, delete it, or revoke Motion & Fitness access in Settings. Revocation does not automatically delete previously saved values. Widget refreshes, hiding old values and system cache cleanup may be delayed.

The app provides bundled artwork and locally generated art cards without requiring access to your entire photo library. When you choose Save & Share, it passes the selected file to the iOS share sheet. Saving to Photos may require permission depending on the operation.

A destination service receives content through its workflow only after you choose a destination app or save location and complete the action. The developer does not automatically publish images or read your social accounts or chats. Recipients handle shared content under their own policies.

Gallery reads the artwork you select through the system Photos picker. The app resizes and re-encodes the image on your device and strips unnecessary source metadata. The resulting image, details, reflection, and signature are stored locally for Gallery and are kept outside shared widget configuration. Delete a saved artwork in Gallery or export it using the system share sheet. The original in Photos remains in place.

4. App Group, local files and retention

Guangyu series may also save schedules, time-display options, birthdays and life-progress parameters for their Lock Screen displays. Removing a widget from the Home or Lock Screen does not delete its saved app configuration. Delete unused items in the corresponding My Widgets category. Membership expiration or revocation does not automatically delete configurations or photos. Remove shortcut bindings in the system widget configuration.

Local data also includes birthdays, anniversaries and display parameters for templates such as Earth Oneline; daily text, nicknames, start dates, series and sizes for Guangyu Lock Screen widgets; Little Sprite Control Center configurations; and themes, artwork bindings, text modes and identifiers for Little Companions. Little Companions uses Apple ActivityKit to display Live Activities on Dynamic Island and the Lock Screen. It uses no remote push notifications and sends no configurations to developer servers. Ending an activity does not delete its saved item. Manage, restart or delete it in My Widgets. Deleting the corresponding saved companion requests that its activity end. The system may end display earlier because of time limits or device conditions.

Some widgets let you configure shortcuts through the system. The system supplies the selected action and display name for display and execution when you tap. This does not grant access to chats, contacts, accounts or other information inside the destination app. That app’s rules apply after it opens. Text, dates and photos on the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, Control Center and Home Screen may be visible to others. Manage your system Lock Screen display settings accordingly.

Preview thumbnails and decoded artwork are used only for on-device display and performance. Gallery “position” means the artwork’s browsing index, not geographic location. Necessary local error logs support troubleshooting; there is no developer remote log collection service. Before voluntarily sending screenshots or logs, remove unrelated personal information.

The main app and its widgets use a system-controlled shared container to store and read configurations, including saved photos, steps and weather. This does not expose data to other developers or apps. Configurations normally remain until edited, deleted or cleared by the system. Gallery preferences contain no personal photos or steps. Exported art cards are written to a local temporary directory, may be overwritten by later exports of the same artwork or cleared by the system, and are not promised to be deleted immediately when the share sheet closes.

Manage and delete saved configurations in My Widgets. Handling of local data after uninstallation depends on iOS and shared-container behavior. Copies in Photos, Files, system backups or third-party platforms are not deleted automatically when you delete within the app; manage them separately. System backup and restoration follow your device settings and Apple’s rules, not an account cloud-sync service supplied by this app.

5. Contacting the developer

Expanding contact details alone sends no data. If you contact the developer by email or WeChat, the developer receives the contact information, problem description and attachments you send, to reply, troubleshoot, handle rights requests or resolve disputes. Provide only what is necessary. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment receipts or unrelated sensitive material.

Contact records are retained as necessary to handle the request, then deleted or de-identified as appropriate, except where law requires retention or records are genuinely needed for a dispute. You may request access, correction or deletion of contact records within the developer’s control. Email and WeChat providers handle communications under their own rules.

6. Third parties and web visits

System services also include WidgetKit Home and Lock Screen widgets, ActivityKit Live Activities and Control Center features. They support on-device presentation and interactions for features you use; they do not open the shared container to other developers. Other Apps download links, artwork sources and licenses, and Apple subscription or refund links access the destination only when you open them. Recipients may receive necessary connection information such as an IP address. This does not let the app read your accounts with those services.

Current services mainly involve Apple location services, WeatherKit, Core Motion Motion & Fitness, the system photo picker, sharing and photo saving, plus GitHub for hosted policy pages. Policies inside Settings are bundled local files. External links use the destination service only when opened. Visiting GitHub or GitHub Pages sends necessary information such as IP address and browser details to the host; see GitHub’s Privacy Statement. These policy pages embed no advertising, third-party analytics scripts or user forms.

Versions offering membership use Apple StoreKit for purchases, restoration and offer-code redemption. The app verifies Apple-signed transactions and subscription validity on the device, and saves expiration, lifetime entitlement, verification time and file version in its shared container for read-only widget entitlement checks. Widget content and photos are not sent to the developer with transactions. Apple handles transactions and payments under its policies. The app receives no Apple Account password, bank-card information or complete payment details. There is no developer membership server or transaction-record upload to a developer backend. We do not sell personal information or proactively disclose local configurations. Where disclosure of information actually held by the developer is legally required, it will be handled within the legally prescribed scope.

7. Security and processing locations

Local configurations stay on your device. System sandboxes, shared-container permissions and system network services help reduce unauthorized access, but no system can be guaranteed absolutely secure. Protect your device unlock credentials and consider what widgets, screenshots and shared content may reveal.

When Apple, GitHub or communication services are involved, information may be processed in regions where those providers operate. We do not promise that all related data is stored in China. If the developer introduces new cross-border provision of personal information, applicable notice, consent and other obligations will be followed.

8. Your rights

You may edit or delete local configurations, manage permissions, stop using features, and contact the developer to request access, copies, correction, deletion or an explanation of handling rules for information actually held by the developer. No account exists to close. The developer cannot remotely read your local configurations or directly delete copies held by third-party platforms.

Requests will be handled within periods required by applicable law. Where identity verification is needed, only proportionate, necessary information will be requested. If a request cannot be fulfilled, reasons and available options will be explained. You retain lawful complaint and remedy rights.

9. Minors

Minors should use the app with guardian guidance. Obtain guardian consent before processing where legally required. Personal information of children under 14 receives special protection under applicable law. Guardians may email access or deletion requests. Avoid unnecessary children’s identity, school or location information in contacts and shares.

10. Changes and contact

If accounts, cloud sync, advertising, analytics or other features change the purposes, scope or recipients of information processing, this policy will be updated and important changes highlighted. Fresh consent will be requested separately where required. This policy does not replace system authorization or separate consent required by law.

Custom artwork imports · November 20, 2026

Custom Gallery imports are stored on your device, not uploaded to a Uafo server or published to other users. They are not desktop widgets. Free users may save up to 5 imported works in total; active members up to 100. After membership expires, existing imports remain available for viewing, editing, image replacement, export and deletion. New imports require fewer than 5 saved works, or renewed membership with room under the 100-work limit.

The app offers no cloud backup, recycle bin or developer-assisted recovery for imported works. Deleting a work in the app or deleting the app and its local data cannot be undone through Uafo; keep your original images and important text separately. Original photos, exported copies and system backups are managed separately. System backup/restore depends on your device settings; we do not guarantee it can recover these records. Local-only storage does not prevent visibility through device access, screenshots or your chosen sharing actions.

The photo picker reads only selected images. The app locally processes image orientation, size and encoding, and stores a display copy, artwork details, source, personal reflections, signature, stable record ID and browsing order in its private app directory, not the Widget shared image configuration. New display copies are limited to a 1600-pixel long edge and 2 MiB, with further downsampling as needed; these are not archival originals. Replacing or deleting a work schedules removal of its old file; temporary exports and system copies may persist separately. Developer access occurs only if you voluntarily send material through support or sharing. Membership diagnostic sharing is user-initiated and includes local purchase verification status and error details, not account passwords or full receipts; check its contents before sending.
